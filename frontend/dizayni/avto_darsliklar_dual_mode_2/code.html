Traffic Signals & Gestures

Master the foundational visual language of the road. Understand every phase of intersection control, from standard tri-color systems to manual traffic controller gestures.

Key Concepts

check_circle

Standard Sequence

Red, Red & Yellow, Green, Flashing Green, Yellow. Understand the transition timings and legal obligations for each phase.

check_circle

Directional Arrows

Green arrows indicate protected turns. Proceed only in the direction indicated when illuminated.

check_circle

Flashing Signals

Flashing red requires a complete stop. Flashing yellow indicates caution; proceed with reduced speed.

check_circle

Pedestrian Phases

Always yield to pedestrians in crosswalks, regardless of parallel vehicle signal status during turns.

warning
gavel Critical Rule

Controller Authority

Manual instructions and gestures provided by authorized traffic controllers (police officers) strictly supercede all electronic traffic signals and static road signs.
